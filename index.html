Message us
Where do you deliver?
Anywhere in Aruba — villas, hotels, beaches and boats. Share your address or a Maps pin and we'll confirm the timing on WhatsApp.
How much notice do you need?
ASAP orders are welcome and depend on availability. For larger or custom boards, a day's notice is ideal.
Can I customise a board?
Absolutely. Allergies, dislikes, extra cheese — add a note to your order or message us and we'll build around you.
How do I pay?
Cash on delivery is easiest — tell us if you need change. Message us on WhatsApp if you'd prefer another arrangement.
Do you cater events?
Yes — villa parties, proposals, brunches and corporate gifts. Send us the details and we'll plan the spread.
